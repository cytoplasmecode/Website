Every site starts somewhere. This one starts here, with the most clichéd title in programming — because sometimes the cliché is exactly right.

## Why bother writing?

A few reasons. Writing forces clarity. You can hold a vague idea in your head for weeks, convinced you understand it, until you try to explain it to someone and the seams start showing. Writing exposes the seams.

It's also a record. I forget things constantly — bugs I've debugged, tradeoffs I've reasoned through, why a system is shaped the way it is. Writing means future-me has a trail to follow.

And honestly? I've learned so much from other people's writing — blog posts, documentation, the occasional well-commented piece of source code. Contributing to that feels worthwhile.

## What to expect

Mostly technical writing. Systems, tools, code. Occasionally something more reflective. I'm not planning to optimize for quantity — I'd rather write one post I'm proud of than five I'm not.

Posts will vary in length and depth. Some will be short notes. Some will go deep. I'll follow the idea where it goes.
